⚠️ Think Before You Allow

A website may request access to your camera, location or files. Never grant permissions unless you understand why they are required and trust the website.

👤 Contacts Awareness

Websites should not be trusted with your contacts simply because they request access. Modern browsers also restrict direct contact access for security reasons.

🔒 Contacts are NOT accessed by this demonstration.

🚨 Awareness Message

Imagine receiving a QR code after a long event. You scan it, open the website and repeatedly click Allow without checking what permissions are being requested.


This demonstration shows why you should stop and think before granting permissions to an unfamiliar website.

✅ Remember

Don't blindly click Allow.

Check the website address, understand the permission being requested and grant access only when necessary.
